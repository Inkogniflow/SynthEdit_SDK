Disperser

A chain of identical 2nd-order allpass filters. The level of every frequency stays the same, but frequencies near the centre frequency are delayed more than the rest, so drums and plucks are smeared into a chirp or "laser" sound. More stages give a stronger effect.

Pins

  • Signal - audio input.
  • Frequency - centre frequency, 1 V/oct, 5 V = 440 Hz. Can be modulated at audio rate.
  • Pinch - how narrow the affected band is. 0 V = wide and smooth (Q 0.5), 10 V = narrow and ringing (Q 32).
  • Amount - number of allpass stages, 0 to 128. 0 bypasses the effect.
  • Mix - 0 V = dry, 10 V = fully dispersed. In between, dry and wet mix to make phaser-like notches.
  • Output - audio output.

CPU use grows with Amount. Modulating Frequency or Pinch costs a little more than fixed values.
